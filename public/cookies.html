Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026. Understand how we use essential cookies and storage mechanisms to optimize session integrity.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are simple text fragments loaded onto your computer or mobile device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make platforms load, function, or communicate status records with background databases securely.

2. How We Use Cookies & Storage

At CreatorChain, we follow a strict **zero-tracking, zero-ad** philosophy. We do not use third-party marketing cookies. We strictly utilize essential cookies and browser Local Storage to enable the following primary features:

  • Session Authentications: Saving your authenticated status when you log in via X (Twitter) or Google so you do not have to reconnect your social profile on every click.
  • Bell Notification Alerts: Retaining your last-checked notification timestamp to alert you precisely when new matching campaigns or dev gigs are launched on the platform.
  • State Settings: Memorizing your preferred user configurations, theme details, and dashboard view filter options.

3. Types of Cookies in Play

All items active on CreatorChain fall under **Strictly Necessary Storage Elements**:

  • Session ID: Temporary storage keys that are deactivated the moment you close your browser tab or sign out.
  • Ecosystem Tokens: Secure local storage records provided by our database layer (Supabase) to keep your authenticated builder profile active and secured against cross-site exploits.

4. Third-Party Connections

While we do not host third-party marketing networks, we connect with verified Web3 vectors to distribute notifications and bounties. For example, our alert engine integrates with **Telegram** for real-time notifications, and we support connecting cryptographic wallets (such as MetaMask or Phantom). These providers utilize their own cookie layers when you launch their corresponding applications or extensions.

5. Managing Your Storage Choices

You can clear, reject, or control local browser storage at any time by accessing your browser's Preferences or Security Settings panel. Please note that disabling essential storage elements may prevent you from logging in, checking submission statuses, or receiving live opportunity alerts.

6. Revisions to Cookie Policy

Any future integrations (such as premium Web3 social analytical partners) will be updated here. We will ensure all future updates maintain our commitment to strict user data respect and privacy.

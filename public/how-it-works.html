FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How is the Builder Reputation score calculated?

Reputation is dynamically calculated based on ecosystem contributions:

  • +100 Points for every successfully approved Opportunity/Bounty submission.
  • +10 Points for posting updates or building in public on the CreatorPulse feed.
  • +1 Point for every single view received on your CreatorPulse posts.
  • 2x Multiplier: Builders who unlock the elite PRO Badge receive a 2x multiplier on all reputation points generated!
What are Builder Badges and how do I unlock them?

We feature two primary trust and experience credentials:

  • VERIFIED Badge: Automatically unlocked when your profile completeness reaches 90% or more (including bios, skills, experience tags, and validated social links).
  • PRO Badge: Awarded to top-tier builders who have successfully submitted, contributed to, or completed 5 or more distinct opportunities on CreatorChain.
How do the real-time notifications work?

Whenever a new opportunity, bounty, or role goes live on CreatorChain, an alert is dispatched instantly across the ecosystem. The notification bell in your header will update dynamically with a neon badge, showing your unread alerts. Clicking any alert inside the dropdown takes you straight to the corresponding opportunity details page instantly.

How does CreatorPulse help me secure gigs?

CreatorPulse is designed for the "Build in Public" ethos. Hiring teams and protocols browse the feed to identify active, self-driven contributors. Showing off real-time updates of your projects, prototypes, or thoughts gets you noticed much faster than a static resume ever could. Additionally, the views and likes you accumulate directly increase your ranking on the global leaderboard.

Are there any fees to list opportunities or apply?

None! CreatorChain is a decentralized ecosystem built to connect builders directly with opportunities without rent-seeking middle-men. Listing roles (both community projects and dedicated team bounties) and applying to opportunities is 100% free of charge.
